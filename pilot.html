This reference checks structured evidence links and supplied approval metadata. It does not determine whether a source document is true or authenticate a reviewer. Customer integration and independent validation remain pilot work.

Four weeks. One agreed scope.

Week 1Define

Choose the workflow, owner, evidence sources and acceptance rules. Record the current baseline.

Week 2Integrate

Connect the agreed input format, review step and evidence record in a bounded pilot environment.

Week 3Challenge

Test missing evidence, stale records, disagreement and prohibited actions.

Week 4Measure

Compare evidence completeness, review time and replay results. Decide whether to expand, revise or stop.

Leave with something your team can inspect

The proposed deliverables are a versioned check for the agreed workflow, positive and negative test cases, an evidence-record demonstration, an operating procedure and results against the agreed measures.

Start with synthetic or approved non-sensitive examples. Scope, timing and fees are agreed before work begins. The pilot evaluates a defined technical control; it does not certify regulatory compliance or replace accountable human review.
